MFP257B1C is a customized thermal gas flow sensor (see Fig.1) based on MEMSIC’s proprietary CMOS technology for thermal mass flow sensing. The monolithic flow sensor chip, mounted on the PCB, can be assembled in different flow channels to be made into different types of flow meters for accurate flow measurement and control. MFP257B1C is designed to use SPI digital communication protocol. It also has a power down mode enabled through the SPI interface for optimal power management. There is a gas temperature sensor integrated in the flow sensor chip to monitor the actual flowing gas temperature, which is used for gas temperature calibration of the flow sensor. The gas temperature data can also be read out 10- bit output also through SPI interface.

Please refer to Fig.2 for MFP257B1C functional block diagram. MFP257B1C operates over supply range from 2.7 to 5.5V.
