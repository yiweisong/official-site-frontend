Why Sensing Matters
From high-density power shelves to backup power systems, current sensing helps manage increasing electrical demands and protect critical equipment.
Higher Current & Power Density
Increasing power levels demand compact, accurate current sensing with minimal conduction losses.
Faster Switching
High-frequency power conversion requires fast current feedback with minimal delay.
Fast Fault Protection
Rapid current detection helps protect critical power electronics from damaging fault conditions.
Accurate Control & Monitoring
Precise current measurements support power regulation, load sharing, and system monitoring.
