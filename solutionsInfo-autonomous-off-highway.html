Why Sensing Matters
Autonomous and off-highway machines depend on robust sensing for navigation continuity, machine control and electrified power conversion across demanding operating environments.
Reliable Navigation & Positioning
Inertial sensing supports positioning continuity when GNSS is degraded or unavailable.
Precise Machine Control
Motion and attitude measurements support vehicle guidance, implement control and operator assistance.
Performance in Harsh Environments
Dependable sensing is essential under vibration, shock, dust and changing temperatures.
Off-Highway Electrification
Accurate current sensing supports efficient motor control, power conversion and protection.
