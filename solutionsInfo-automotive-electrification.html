Automotive & Electrification
Precision current and inertial sensing for electric, connected and increasingly autonomous vehicles.
The Challenge
Why Sensing Matters
From high-voltage power conversion to autonomous navigation, accurate sensing supports efficient operation, system protection and reliable vehicle motion.
Higher Efficiency & Driving Range
Accurate current sensing supports efficient power conversion, battery management, and motor control.
Faster Switching & Control
Advanced power electronics demand fast current feedback with minimal delay for precise control.
Electrical Safety & Protection
Isolated current measurement and rapid fault detection help protect high-voltage vehicle power systems.
Reliable Motion & Positioning
Precise inertial measurements support vehicle dynamics and positioning when external navigation signals are degraded.
