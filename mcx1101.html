The MCx1101 family is a high-accuracy, galvanically isolated current-sensor platform based on ACEINNA’s AMR sensing technology. With 5 MHz bandwidth, 80 ns response time, and low input-referred noise of 35 µA/√Hz, the family enables precise measurement of fast current dynamics in power-conversion, control, monitoring, and protection applications.

AC or DC current flows through the integrated primary conductor, generating a magnetic field that is measured by the AMR sensing element. Integrated signal-conditioning circuitry converts the magnetic signal into an analog output proportional to the measured current. The family includes MCA1101 absolute-gain and MCR1101 ratiometric versions, with 3.3 V and 5 V supply options and ±5 A, ±20 A, ±50 A and ±65 A current ranges.

The MCx1101 family also provides a precision reference output and fast fault output for system monitoring and protection. Reinforced galvanic isolation, together with UL 1577 and IEC/EN 62368-1 certification, supports use in demanding industrial power systems.
